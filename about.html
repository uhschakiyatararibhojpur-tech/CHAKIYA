About Our School

UHS Chakiya Tarari, Bhojpur, Bihar

Welcome to Our School

UHS Chakiya Tarari is committed to providing quality education, developing strong values, encouraging creativity, and helping every student achieve their full potential.

Our Vision

To develop knowledgeable, confident, responsible and compassionate students who can contribute positively to society. We aspire to create an inclusive learning environment where every child has the opportunity to grow and succeed.

Our Mission

To provide meaningful and accessible education through dedicated teaching, practical learning, discipline, innovation and equal opportunities. We aim to nurture curiosity, good character and lifelong learning among our students.

Our Objectives

  • Provide quality education to every student.
  • Develop discipline, honesty, respect and responsibility.
  • Encourage scientific thinking and practical learning.
  • Improve digital literacy and computer education.
  • Promote sports, creativity and extracurricular activities.
  • Maintain a safe, inclusive and supportive school environment.
  • Build cooperation between teachers, parents and students.
  • Prepare students for higher education and responsible citizenship.

हमारा उद्देश्य

  • प्रत्येक विद्यार्थी को गुणवत्तापूर्ण शिक्षा प्रदान करना।
  • विद्यार्थियों में अनुशासन, ईमानदारी और जिम्मेदारी विकसित करना।
  • विज्ञान, तकनीकी शिक्षा और व्यावहारिक ज्ञान को बढ़ावा देना।
  • कंप्यूटर शिक्षा और डिजिटल साक्षरता का विकास करना।
  • खेलकूद, रचनात्मकता और सह-पाठ्यक्रम गतिविधियों को प्रोत्साहित करना।
  • विद्यालय में सुरक्षित और सहयोगपूर्ण वातावरण बनाए रखना।
  • विद्यार्थियों को उच्च शिक्षा और जिम्मेदार नागरिकता के लिए तैयार करना।
