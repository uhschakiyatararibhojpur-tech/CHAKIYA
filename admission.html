Admission Information

UHS Chakiya Tarari, Bhojpur, Bihar

Welcome to our school

Classes

For admission availability and class-wise details, please contact the school office.

Classes: 9 to 12 (subject to current school offerings and seat availability).

Documents to Bring

  • Previous class marksheet or school certificate
  • Transfer Certificate (TC), if applicable
  • Student's Aadhaar card, if required
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Parent or guardian details

Please confirm the required documents with the school office before submitting them.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the school office.
  2. Ask for the admission form for the relevant class.
  3. Fill in the student's details carefully.
  4. Attach the required documents.
  5. Submit the form to the school office for verification.

Important Information

Admission dates, eligibility, fees and seat availability should be confirmed directly with the school office.
